Research Interests

  • Multi-agent control: bearing- and angle-based formation control, network localization, matrix-weighted and matrix-scaled consensus
  • Resilient control: formation control and localization under denial-of-service attacks
  • Nonlinear and intelligent control: adaptive, robust and reinforcement-learning-based control
  • Missile guidance and control: autopilot design and guidance algorithms for flight vehicles

Industry projects at Viettel Aerospace Institute (VTX)

  • Dec 2023 – Aug 2024: Reinforcement-learning-based adaptive tuning law for a three-loop autopilot. Applied DDPG within the three-loop autopilot framework, designed training scenarios, and compared against gain scheduling via Monte Carlo evaluation.
  • Jul 2022 – Jul 2023: Adaptive controllers for roll stabilization of flight vehicles. Flight dynamics modeling, adaptive roll-stabilization control design, and Monte Carlo evaluation against traditional controllers.

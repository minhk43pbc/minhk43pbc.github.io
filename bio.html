Biography

Nhat-Minh Le-Phan (Lê Phan Nhật Minh) received his B.Sc. (2022, 5-year program) and M.Sc. (2024) degrees in Control and Automation Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), graduating as valedictorian of both programs (B.Sc. rank: 1/496). From 2022 to 2024 he worked as a Guidance and Control Engineer at Viettel Aerospace Institute (VTX), Hanoi, Vietnam. Since August 2024 he has been a faculty member (Junior Lecturer) of the Department of Automation Engineering at HUST. Since August 2025 he has been a PhD student in Automatic Control in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, under the supervision of Prof. Lihua Xie.

His research interests include multi-agent control, resilient control, nonlinear and intelligent control, and missile guidance and control.

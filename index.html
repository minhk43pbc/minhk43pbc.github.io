About me

I am a PhD student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, working with Prof. Xie Lihua since August 2025, and a Junior Lecturer in the Department of Automation Engineering at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Previously, I spent two years as a Guidance and Control Engineer at Viettel Aerospace Institute (VTX). My research is in control theory, with a focus on multi-agent systems, networked control, resilient control, and learning-based control.
